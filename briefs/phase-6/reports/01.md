## Open problems

- **Pass 1 was killed.** With `MAX_SOURCES = 100` and no time limit, 10 domains (large sitemap indexes
  on slow hosts, 20 s timeout each) kept the pool alive past 18 min. Fixed by `MAX_SOURCES = 1000`, an
  early stop once the in-domain URL count reaches `MAX_URLS`, and a `DOMAIN_BUDGET` of 5 min per domain;
  pass 2/3 then completed in 162 s / 302 s. 3 domains still end at the budget.
- **`substack.com` is the weakest file**: 249 of its (thousands of) child sitemaps were fetched before
  the budget stopped it; 27,374 of the URLs seen were dropped as foreign (`*.substack.com` is not the
  domain or `www.` + domain), leaving 611 in-domain URLs. Re-running would not help — 2 req/s ×
  thousands of children is over any reasonable budget.
- **Truncation at 50,000 URLs is per the brief**, but 30 domains are affected; brief 02/04 should treat
  `truncated: true` as "not the whole site".
- **Blocked (3) and failed (3) domains are worth a retry later** — the 403s may be UA/geo dependent, and
  the 3 failures were transient timeouts.
- `catalog/corpus/MANIFEST.md` was regenerated by the `bun tools/fetch.mjs --sitemaps` smoke run (fetch.mjs
  always rewrites it from what is on disk). 784 rows now carry kind `sitemap`; other agents' rows are
  unchanged in content.
- `bun test ./tools` initially had 9 pre-existing failures in this fresh worktree because the gitignored
  `catalog/items/` and `catalog/search-index.json` did not exist; I ran `bun tools/index.mjs` (not
  `build.mjs`) to generate them, after which all 72 tests pass. Those artifacts are not committed.
- `--only=…` merges into an existing `catalog/sitemaps-report.json` instead of replacing it, so a partial
  run cannot wipe the full report; a bare full run replaces it.
- Politeness is per request URL host (redirect targets are not separately rate-limited), and per-host
  blocking counts `robots.txt` responses too — both are deliberate, neither caused a loss here.
