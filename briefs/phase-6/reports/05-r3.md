## Open problems

- **25 of 28 domains are not component or example sites** (icon libraries, portfolios, product/marketing
  sites, asset feeds, job boards) — that split looks like what this batch was meant to find, but it means
  only ~134 new items come out of 28 domains.
- **Template shops were skipped, not mapped** (`lexingtonthemes.com`, `kairoui.online`). The catalogue does
  auto-map some `/template/{name}` families (`gettemplate.app`, `astryx.atmeta.com`), so if the maintainer
  wants full-site themes in scope these two should be revisited (`/templates/{name}`, 71 and 13 pages).
- **`logs.run` keeps its own host in item URLs** while every request 302s to `data-table.openstatus.dev`
  (the demo's canonical host, not a corpus domain). Items are stable and 200, but if that host is later
  added to the corpus it should own these pages instead.
- **`getlayers.ai` is a judgement call**: 18 of 345 layer pages tag to an element (background 5, 3d 3, card 3,
  carousel 2, …) and the `/sections` category is real UI; the library as a whole is generated visual assets,
  so it was skipped rather than mapped as an element-less feed.
- **`hero.gallery` tag pages are the only kind filters**, and only 4 of 127 tags (`centered-hero`,
  `split-grid`, `video-element`, `social-proof-element`) map to a taxonomy variant; tags like
  `full-width`/`inset-layout`/`media-gallery-element` have no hero variant to attach to.
- **`getcssscan.com` sits at 50% element coverage** because two of its four galleries (`/css-box-shadow-examples`,
  `/css-shapes`) are CSS-feature galleries with no taxonomy element.
- **A fresh worktree fails 9 pre-existing tests** in `tools/items.test.mjs` until the gitignored generated
  artifacts exist; `bun tools/index.mjs` (not `build.mjs`) regenerated `catalog/items/` (52,910 items) and
  `catalog/search-index.json` (27.5 MB), after which all 100 tests pass. Those artifacts are gitignored and
  were not committed.
