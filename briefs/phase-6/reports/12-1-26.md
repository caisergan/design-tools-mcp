## Decisions worth recording

- **a-fresh.website** — only `/components/<site-or-site+element>` (one showcased web component, "a-fresh |
  Joinwrap web component") and `/popups/<brand>` are clean leaves; `/components/<element>` pages (hero, faq,
  features, footer, pricing, stats, team, testimonials, bento-grid, brands, call-to-action) are element
  listings, excluded as items but wired up as `elements_from`, which lifts element coverage to 84%.
  `/websites/<x>`, `/pages/<x>` and `/emails/<x>` put category listings ("The Best SaaS Website Examples",
  "Abandoned Cart Email Marketing Examples") and leaf showcases at the same depth, and the root namespace
  holds ~1,000 user/spam slugs — all left out rather than guessed.
- **freebiesui.com** — five tool families × six `/…ui-kits|app-designs|mockups|web-templates|icons|wireframing`
  category pages (24, excluded) with leaves at `/<family>/<name>` and `/<family>/<category>/<name>`; the ~1,086
  `/attachment/<file>` pages are per-file pages. The host also serves injected SEO spam (`/all-freebies/<casino
  article>`, `/bh*`, `/bahis*`, Turkish gambling articles) — not in the pattern, and `/tag/*` (2,542) are tags.
- **bestwebsite.gallery** — `/sites/<creator|framework|service|country|style|cms|color>/<value>` (296) read as
  facet pages ("Style Right", "Framework NuxtJS") linking to `/sites/sotd/<Y>/<M>/<D>/<name>` showcase pages, so
  only the dated archive is matched; `/links/*` (2,784) is a link blog.
- **designfuel.com** — `/inspiration/sections/<type>` (29) are section-type listings; 16 of them map to
  taxonomy elements and feed `elements_from` (hero, faq, features, footer, form, pricing, navbar, cta, logos,
  carousel, stats, tables, tabbed-content, team, testimonials, timeline). The other 13 (careers, comparison,
  content-block, featured-posts, image-gallery, post-header, pricing-calculator, signup, steps, text-block,
  video-block, blog-archive, tabbed-content duplicates) have no element id and are not wired.
- **foliobin.com** counted as inspiration: each `/portfolio/<designer>` is a full-page-screenshot showcase of one
  portfolio site ("Alexander Vilinskyy — Digital Designer"), not a bare profile — the page carries the
  screenshots, a Visit link and the portfolio's metadata.
- **appshots.design** is included on structural evidence (sitemap slugs are app names; site copy: "Real world UX
  design inspirations"; `/category/*` taxonomy) because the SPA answers every plain `curl` with the same 5 KB
  shell — no browser/impersonation allowed, so titles could not be verified. Its 110 flow pages share one path
  (`/flows?id=…&slug=…`) and are not addressable as items.
- **dribbble.com** is a skip, not a pattern: the downloaded sitemap (truncated at the 50k cap) contains no shot
  pages at all — 49,890 `/search/<query>` and 110 `/hire/<role>` URLs. A re-crawl with the shots sitemap would
  be needed.
- **Element coverage is naturally low** for showcase galleries (~1–9%): slugs are brand names ("Aloe", "Mistral"),
  not UI element names. It is 0% for foliobin (designer names) and freefaces (typeface names) — expected.

## Open problems

1. **a-fresh.website's `/websites`, `/pages`, `/emails` (2,400+ URLs) are unmapped.** They are the site's main
   website-inspiration families, but categories and leaves share one depth/namespace and there is no category
   index URL that plain `curl` can read (the `/websites` index is client-rendered; the category list only
   appears mixed into each category page's nav). Making them items needs either a per-URL check of ~1,000 pages
   or a category allow/deny list from the coordinator.
2. **appshots.design could not be page-verified** (client-rendered SPA, browser fetches forbidden by the brief).
   The 491 items rest on the sitemap + site copy; if a rendered check later contradicts that, the pattern is the
   thing to revisit.
3. **freebiesui.com's attachment pages (~1,086) and `/games|spiele|giochi|spellen` (98) were left out**; if the
   coordinator wants per-file download pages as items, they are a separate, deliberate call (machine-shaped
   `/<freebie>/attachment/<file>` pages).
4. **admiretheweb.com is 18 items under the 3,000 cap**; any new shows push it over — the next decision there is
   whether showed sites stay one family or split.
5. **`bun test tools/patterns.test.mjs`**: 27 pass / 0 fail. The full `bun test tools/` suite still has the 9
   pre-existing `mcp/items/build` failures that need `catalog/items/`, which this brief forbids building.
