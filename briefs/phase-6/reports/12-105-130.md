## Notes / judgement calls

1. **Several rows carried a `primary` that does not fit the site** (the aggregator's keyword categories):
   yashbanka.com ("fonts" — it is a portfolio), and chrisgregori.dev, learnify.shefali.dev, frontendmentor.io,
   brandbird.app, color.adobe.com, supermotion.co, aiselfie.studio, toggles.colorion.co ("color-effects" — blog,
   tutorials, practice platform, screenshot tool, Adobe app, video tool, AI photo app, one-page toggle library).
   Each is skipped with what the site is, except apple-summary-slides.vercel.app, whose event pages are genuine
   slide-design inspiration and are mapped despite the queue category (noted in the file).
2. **Whole-site families over the 3,000 cap** got the `needs coordinator decision` skip the brief prescribes:
   whatfontis.com (~49,927 font pages, also truncated), color-name.com (12,642 named colours), ui.jln.dev
   (16,307 themes). None has a narrower sub-family to keep.
3. **Where a "family" would produce garbage names it is skipped, not mapped**: colordesigner.io's 736 palette
   pages have human titles ("Charcoal Rose") but hex-only slugs, and coolors.co's 50k pages are pure hex pages —
   both `generated colours`.
4. **youworkforthem.com `{*}` / cssgradient.io literals** are deliberate: `{*}` keeps the T-number out of the
   item name (`Neonbox`, not `T17519 Neonbox`), and the 7 cssgradient pages are literal templates so the names
   read "Shades Of Red" instead of just "Red" (`{name}` would capture only the colour word).
5. **No `variants_from` / `elements_from` anywhere**: the only fixed element used is `background`, which has no
   variants in the taxonomy, and the icon/font/colour families name no element (per brief 12, that is fine).
6. **Single-page apps** (mycolor.space, shadesigner.com, supa-palette.com) and the two one-page colorion
   libraries got the standard "sitemap is only the landing page" skip; the colorion pages were checked for
   in-page anchors first (one grid id each, nothing per item, no `href="#…"` links).
