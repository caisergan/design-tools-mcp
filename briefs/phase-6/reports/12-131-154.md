## Open problems / judgement calls

- **webzooo.com** over the cap: `/site/{name}` is 4,009 showcased-site pages; there is no more specific
  family to keep, so the skip is the brief's `needs coordinator decision` message.
- **refero `/style/<uuid>` (1,342 pages).** They are the site's main content (“Grafana Labs design
  system”, h1 server-rendered) but the slug is a UUID and `sitemapScan` names items from URL segments, so
  the items would be UUID-named and unfindable by name. Excluded; a name-from-`<title>`/h1 source in the
  scanner would unlock them. The 51 named guide pages are mapped.
- **dialectcn.xyz / shadcnpreset.com** are the same pattern of problem on preset pages (`/feed/<id>`,
  `/preset/<id>`): fine pages, opaque ids, names only in page titles/JS → skips, not id-named items.
- **designmd.ai caps at 400 items** because `--fetch-filters` fetches at most 20 listing pages (21 exist).
  A deeper pager or a second `urls_from` page (`/explore?page=21`) would close the ~20-item gap.
- **getdesign.md** merges 15 same-brand URL pairs (`/design-md/cursor` ÷ `/cursor/design-md`) into one item
  each via the scanner's id dedupe; both URLs serve 200.
- **m3.material.io scope**: foundations/styles guideline pages are included (they are the site's guidelines
  content); `/develop/*` implementation docs, `/blog/*` and the root section indexes are not.
- **ai-interaction.com is client-rendered** (Vite SPA): plain curl sees only the shell. The task pages were
  confirmed via the sitemap (64 `/atlas/task/*`) and the app's task data chunks (ai/human/system tasks);
  item names come from the slugs (“Task Detect”).
- **uicolors.app** served one transient Cloudflare “Just a moment…” challenge to the first request, then
  behaved normally on retries — treated as reachable, not “blocked”.
