## Decisions worth recording

- **Over-cap families got the coordinator skip, not a silent subset.** The five sites whose right family exceeds
  the 3,000 cap (`needs coordinator decision: …`) name the exact families and counts so the coordinator can either
  lift the cap or pick a subset; e.g. `interfaceingame.com` 17,279 `/screenshots/*` vs 403 `/games/*` hubs,
  `pageflows.com` 8,590 flows vs 974 email galleries + 1,195 product pages. `minimal.gallery` misses the cap by 11
  (3,011 showcase pages) — same treatment rather than dropping 11 real pages or blowing the cap.
- **lapa.ninja is Cloudflare-blocked** (`Attention Required!`, 403 on `/post/proof-2/`, `/typeface/switzervariable/`,
  `/elements/zentry/`); its `/post` family (7,503) would also exceed the cap.
- **`pafolios.com` has no element** (0%) — portfolio pages carry no taxonomy element, same as
  `h1gallery.com` (1%), `ogimage.gallery` (2%), `rebrand.gallery` (0%) and `save.design` (1%). Not forced from slugs.
- **Font-usage pages were skipped on the three inspiration sites** (`seesaw.website` `/font/*` 630,
  `save.design` `/fonts/*` 258, `saaslandingpage.com` `/font/*` 401): they are 'websites using this font' facets
  of their own catalogs, not font family pages; the queue lists these sites as inspiration only.
- **landingfolio kind pages**: `/library/<kind>` = production-ready components, `/components/<kind>` = curated
  real-site sections. 16 kinds exist under both prefixes (pricing, footer, hero, faq, …) → 126 matched URLs,
  **110 items**, 16 collapsed (first URL in sitemap order wins; here the `/components/` URL). Same id-rule collapse
  on **rebrand.gallery**: 752 matched, **726 items**, 26 brands (`7up`, `bugatti`, …) exist as `/rebrand/…` and `/bento/…`.
- **`saaslandingpage.com` bare-slug collection pages excluded** ('64 Best Pricing Page Examples', '51 Best About Us
  Page Examples' — confirmed by <title> for `/pricing`, `/about-us`, `/contact-us`), as are `/blog`, `/faq`, `/privacy`,
  `/testimonials`, `/features`, `/templates`, `/fonts`, `/tags`.
- **`gridddy.framer.website` is a CTA gallery**, not a generic showcase: home meta says "the best pre-footer CTA
  design practices across the web" and `/sites/charter` HTML contains 'pre-footer'/'CTA' → `element: "cta"` fixed.
- Granularity: `example` for one showcased item per page (galleries, templates); `page` for landingfolio's
  kind pages (component-library pages). All patterns are `access: "page"`.
- Not touched although adjacent: `/analyze` (save.design, same 204 slugs as `/website`), `/academy` (scrolltide
  tutorials), `/tailwind` (landings.dev CSS reference), `/store/templates/<hex>` (landingfolio store, hex-only slugs).
