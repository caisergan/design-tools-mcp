## Notes and open problems

1. **uidesigndaily.com** — the site's own `sitemap.xml` (21 URLs) lists only marketing/auth pages; the design pages
   (`/posts/<name>`, ~1500) are reached through the Webflow paginated homepage (`?e326175c_page=N`). 16 homepage pages
   were fetched (the 20-page cap of `--fetch-filters` was not hit; page 16 is the last). Items are a snapshot: rerun
   `--fetch-filters --only=uidesigndaily.com --refresh` to pick up newer designs.
2. **webpo.space** — 799 matching URLs give 788 items: 11 brand slugs exist in two families (e.g.
   `/best-app-gallery/popcorn` and `/best-og-image-inspiration/popcorn`), and the adapter keeps one item per slug
   ("two pages whose segment slugs the same are one item").
3. **webdesign-inspiration.com** — one example family only (`/web-design/<showcased-website>`, 3785 URLs incl. ~88
   duplicate slugs), over the 3,000 per-site cap, so per brief 12 it is a `"needs coordinator decision"` skip rather
   than a pattern. It also answers plain curl with HTTP 403 (Cloudflare), so its pages could not be spot-checked.
4. **Icon sites with only glyph pages are skip files** (boxicons, css.gg, favicon.io, icons.getbootstrap.com), matching
   the existing hand skips for the same shape (`lineicons.com`, `itshover.com`, `animateicons.in`).
5. **hugeicons.com kept as items** — the 10 `/icons/<style>` pages are browsable set pages ("Solid Sharp Icons for
   Dashboards … explore thousands of icons"), which is exactly brief 12's "one icon set/pack/library page". The
   product's own pages (pricing, FAQ, packages, integrations, blog) are not matched.
6. **icoon.co / 3dicons.co** — pack/collection pages are kept (30 + 4); single assets and single 3D icons are treated
   as glyph pages per brief 12 and are not matched.
7. No `--fetch-filters` was needed anywhere else; every other pattern is sitemap-only. No `catalog/corpus/` file was
   added to git.
